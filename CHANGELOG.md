# Changelog

## 0.7.1
Bubble label is a small flag across the bubble's left edge that slides out and back with a spring and tilt.

## 0.7.0
First-visit tour: a bubble from the bell, then one per step a site adds (`ace_nudge_onboarding`).

## 0.6.2
Notification bubble shows its time left as a bar and holds while pointed at. Sites can colour it with custom properties.

## 0.6.1
The header bell's place is held from the footer while the page is parsed.

## 0.6.0
Other plugins can offer their own opt-in alerts (`ace_nudge_site_follows`).

## 0.5.0
Bet slip reminder is opt-in and sent once before the first event.

## 0.4.x
Notification feed beside the bell, in-place refresh once a minute, notification sounds that can be switched off, bubble timing and test-mode push guard.

## 0.3.x
Browser API for other scripts, alerts managed in a site's settings panel, minimum age treated as a number (ac3f4e7), team colours on follow buttons.

## 0.2 and earlier
Opt-in browser notifications and newsletter, per-site age limit, WooCommerce order updates and email choices, follows that lapse, bet slip reminders.
